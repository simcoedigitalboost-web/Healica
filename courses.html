Continuing Education

Expand your skills with hands-on certification courses taught by experienced practitioners.

Cupping Therapy Certification

$300
8 Hours | Full Day

Cupping therapy is an ancient healing practice that has gained popularity in modern therapeutic settings. This hands-on certification course teaches the art and science of cupping, allowing you to integrate this powerful modality into your practice.

What You'll Learn

  • History and theory of cupping therapy
  • Types of cups and cupping techniques (static, sliding, flash)
  • Indications and contraindications
  • Treatment protocols for common conditions
  • Safety considerations and aftercare
  • Hands-on practice with feedback

Who Should Attend

Registered Massage Therapists, Physiotherapists, Chiropractors, Acupuncturists, and other healthcare professionals looking to expand their treatment toolkit.

Kinesiology Taping Certification

$300
8 Hours | Full Day

Kinesiology tape is widely used in sports medicine and rehabilitation to support muscles, reduce pain, and improve movement patterns. This certification course provides comprehensive training in assessment and application techniques.

What You'll Learn

  • Principles of kinesiology taping
  • Tape properties and application fundamentals
  • Muscle facilitation and inhibition techniques
  • Lymphatic and postural corrections
  • Sport-specific applications
  • Common injury protocols (shoulder, knee, ankle, back)
  • Hands-on practice with multiple techniques

Who Should Attend

Massage Therapists, Athletic Therapists, Physiotherapists, Chiropractors, and fitness professionals working with athletes or rehabilitation clients.

Hot Stone Therapy Certification

$275
4 Hours | Half Day

Hot stone massage combines the therapeutic benefits of heat with skilled massage techniques to create a deeply relaxing and healing experience. Learn to safely and effectively integrate heated stones into your massage practice.

What You'll Learn

  • Types of stones and their properties
  • Proper heating and temperature safety
  • Stone placement techniques
  • Integration with Swedish and deep tissue massage
  • Contraindications and precautions
  • Full-body hot stone treatment protocol

Who Should Attend

Registered Massage Therapists looking to add a popular and profitable service to their treatment menu.

Indie Head Massage Certification

$200
4 Hours | Half Day

Indian Head Massage is a traditional Ayurvedic practice focusing on the head, neck, shoulders, and face. This deeply relaxing treatment is excellent for stress relief, headache management, and promoting mental clarity.

What You'll Learn

  • History and origins of Indian Head Massage
  • Ayurvedic principles and energy balance
  • Techniques for scalp, face, neck, and shoulders
  • Pressure points and marma therapy basics
  • Seated and table-based treatment protocols
  • Oil vs. dry treatment approaches

Who Should Attend

Massage Therapists, Estheticians, and wellness practitioners looking for a versatile treatment that can be performed in various settings.

Aromatherapy Make & Take

$175
2 Hours

Discover the therapeutic power of essential oils in this interactive workshop. Learn the fundamentals of aromatherapy and create your own custom blends to take home and use in your practice or personal wellness routine.

What You'll Learn

  • Introduction to essential oils and their properties
  • Safety guidelines and dilution ratios
  • Blending for specific therapeutic goals
  • Carrier oils and application methods
  • Integration with massage therapy
  • Create custom blends to take home

Who Should Attend

Anyone interested in aromatherapy, from healthcare professionals to wellness enthusiasts. No prior experience required.
