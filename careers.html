Join Our Team

Build your practice with Healica. We're growing and looking for passionate healthcare professionals.

Why Healica?

At Healica, we believe in supporting our practitioners so they can focus on what they do best: helping patients heal. We handle the business side so you can build a thriving practice without the administrative headaches.

Registered Massage Therapist (RMT)

We're seeking Registered Massage Therapists to join our growing team at our Barrie and Orillia locations. Whether you're newly graduated or experienced, we provide the support you need to build a successful practice.

What We Offer

  • 70/30 compensation split
  • Employee option available
  • Established patient base with consistent bookings
  • Full administrative support
  • Direct billing to 50+ insurance companies
  • Online booking system
  • Modern, professional clinic environment
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Continuing education opportunities
  • Supportive team atmosphere

Requirements

  • Current registration with CMTO (College of Massage Therapists of Ontario)
  • Professional liability insurance
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Commitment to patient care and professional development

Nice to Have

  • Experience with specialized modalities (cupping, hot stone, sports massage)
  • Existing patient base (not required)
  • Interest in continuing education

Ready to apply?

Send your resume and cover letter to:

cristie@healicanaturals.com or jason@healicanaturals.com

About Our Clinics

Healica Massage Therapy has been serving Simcoe County since 2015. With two locations in Barrie and Orillia, we've built a reputation for quality care and a supportive work environment.

Our Values

  • Patient Care First - We prioritize the wellbeing of every patient
  • Professional Excellence - We maintain the highest standards in our practice
  • Supportive Environment - We help each other succeed
  • Continuous Learning - We invest in ongoing education and development
  • Environmental Responsibility - We use natural products and sustainable practices
